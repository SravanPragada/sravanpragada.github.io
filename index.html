Digital shopping cart

Final-year major project, SRKR Engineering College, Dec 2023 – Apr 2024

A smart cart that knows what you put in it. A Raspberry Pi runs the system, an ESP32 handles Wi-Fi, a camera reads each product's barcode, and ADC-based weight sensors confirm the item actually went into the cart. Stock updates the moment an item is placed.

At checkout, the shopper scans a QR code that opens their payment app directly, so there's no billing counter. I validated the system with 15 different products in a real test environment, then handed it off to junior students, who are extending it into a handheld basket.

  • Raspberry Pi
  • ESP32
  • Python
  • Barcode camera
  • Load cells / ADC
  • Wi-Fi
