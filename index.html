Smart lab room

AICTE Idea Lab, 2022 – 2024

Automated a physical lab room with Arduino and C++. Motion, sound, touch, light, distance, IR, water-level, and temperature sensors control the lighting, taps, and outdoor lights. Digital shopping cart

Final-year project, Dec 2023 – Apr 2024

A cart that identifies products and updates stock as items go in. A Raspberry Pi runs the system, an ESP32 handles Wi-Fi, a camera reads barcodes, and ADC-based weight sensors confirm what was placed. A QR code at checkout opens the shopper's payment app, so there's no billing queue. After testing in a real store setting, I handed it off to juniors, who are extending it to handheld baskets.
